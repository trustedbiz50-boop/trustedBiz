Frequently asked questions

How does billing work?

Plans are per project. Free is free. Paid is UGX {{ '{:,}'.format(hosting_fee_monthly or 15000) }} a month or UGX {{ '{:,}'.format(hosting_fee_yearly or 150000) }} a year. You request an upgrade in your project's settings, pay by MTN or Airtel Money on WhatsApp, and we switch the plan on when we see the payment.

What happens if I don't open my dashboard?

A Free project pauses after 90 days without a dashboard visit, and visitors see a paused page. Opening your dashboard brings it straight back. We remind you a week before. Paid projects never pause.

Can I leave and take my site with me?

Yes. Your files are yours. Keep your source in Git or your own ZIP and you can host it anywhere. We never delete a project without you asking, apart from unpaid Paid projects after a grace period.

Can I run Python or Node apps today?

Not yet. You can upload them and they are stored safely, then start running automatically when app hosting launches. Until then only static sites are served.

What payment methods do you accept?

MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money, arranged on WhatsApp. Card payments are not available yet.

Do you offer refunds?

All payments are non-refundable unless stated otherwise at purchase. See the Terms.
