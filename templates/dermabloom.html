Our Philosophy

Where Science

Derma Bloom Aesthetics & Wellness Spa is a premium beauty destination in Kisubi-Katabi along Entebbe Road. We exist at the intersection of advanced skincare science and the art of relaxation.

Our highly trained aestheticians don't just treat your skin | they transform it. Using only premium, dermatologist-approved products selected specifically for African skin, every visit delivers visible, lasting results.

From our elegant reception lounge to our private treatment rooms, every detail at Derma Bloom is designed to make you feel what luxury actually feels like.
